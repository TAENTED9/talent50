01— The opportunity

Strong at the top. Strong at the bottom. Hollow in between.

Africa produces world-class talent the world pays a fortune for, but only once it leaves. Grassroots receives less than 5% of national sports budgets, and the sector runs on almost no fan data. That gap is the opportunity.

The top
Marquee events & exported stars
The missing middle
Leagues, distribution, data, capital
The connective layer that doesn't exist yet, and where we build.
The bottom
225M+ players, youngest population on earth
Talent leaves early, cheap.
The average outbound transfer fee is a fraction of what the same player commands at a top-tier league.
Value is captured abroad.
The platforms, the rights and the fan relationships are built somewhere else.
The home market is unbuilt.
600M+ fans, almost entirely unmonetised, because no one has built the connective layer.
